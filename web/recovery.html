VISTACrowd Safety Module

Recovery question

Set a question only you can answer. If you forget your password, the Forgot password page asks it and lets you choose a new password.

  • Pick something only you know. Colours, city names and your own name are easy to guess.
  • Writing your own question, like a private backup phrase, is safer than the common ones.
  • Answers need at least 6 characters, or 10 for admin and security accounts. Capital letters and extra spaces do not matter.
  • Five wrong answers lock recovery for 15 minutes.
