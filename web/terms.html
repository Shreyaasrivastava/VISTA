VISTA does not replace the police, fire service, ambulance or venue staff. In an emergency, call 112 or ask venue staff for help. Do not wait for a VISTA alert before acting on what you can see around you.

Accuracy of alerts

Crowd levels are estimates made by analysing camera images. They can be late, incomplete or wrong, for example when a camera is blocked, badly lit or not working. An alert not arriving does not mean an area is safe.
