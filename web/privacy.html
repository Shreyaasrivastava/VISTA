Who we are

The VISTA Crowd Safety Module ("VISTA", "we") is operated by [[FILL: legal name of the operator]]. You can contact us about privacy at [[FILL: privacy contact email]].

What we collect

  • Account details. A username and password for admin, security and attendee accounts. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • Registration details. Your name, phone number, the venue you register for, whether you registered in advance or on the spot, a registration code, and your check-in time.
  • Location. Only if you turn on location alerts. We use it to work out which zone of a venue you are in so we can warn you about crowding there. [[FILL: say whether location is stored and for how long]]
  • Camera analysis. Cameras at a venue are used to estimate how many people are in each zone. VISTA does not use facial recognition and does not identify individuals. [[FILL: say whether video frames are stored and for how long]]
  • Technical data. The web server records your IP address and the time of each request for security and fault finding. This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. It loads the Noto Serif Thai font from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

Why we use it

  • To create and manage your registration and QR code.
  • To show venue staff how crowded each zone is and to send alerts.
  • To keep a record of alerts and who acknowledged them.
  • To protect the service from misuse.

Consent

We process your registration details and location with your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before you withdrew. We handle personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where it applies.

Who sees your data

The admin and security staff of the venue you register for can see your registration details. An admin can only see data for their own venue. Notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging, a service run by Google. We do not sell personal data.
