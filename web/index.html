What VISTA does

Three jobs, one system.

Cameras? Counted.

VISTA finds where each person stands on the floor and counts the people inside a zone, using the cameras the venue already has.

Zones? Ranked.

Each zone shows a level (Normal, Caution, Warning or Critical) based on how full it is compared with its capacity.

Venues? Separate.

Every venue admin sees only their own zones and cameras. Security staff see the current level of every zone at their venue.

How it works

From a camera image to a crowd level in four steps.

  1. A camera watches one zone.For example a railway platform, a temple courtyard or a stadium gate.
  2. VISTA counts the people inside it.It finds each person's feet on the floor and counts the ones inside the zone's marked area.
  3. The count becomes a level.The count is compared with what the zone can safely hold, and the zone gets one of four levels.
  4. Staff see it, and soon get alerts.Admins and security watch the levels on a dashboard. Alerts to staff and visitor phones are in development.

Crowd levels

Default limits. Each camera can set its own.

  • Normal under 50% full
  • Caution 50 to 75% full
  • Warning 75 to 90% full
  • Critical 90% full or more
